Research date: 2026-10-15. Almost all sourced material on the fruit-drama genre dates from **late Feb – Apr 2026** (the genre's peak). I found **no sourced reporting on new AI-story formats launched in Sep–Oct 2026**. Treat everything below as "spring 2026 state of the art" unless a date says otherwise. ## Q1. Hook formulas and plot structures used by the most-viewed AI fruit/food dramas and AI micro-dramas (concrete examples, view counts, URLs)

### Takeaway
The genre has two winning templates. (a) The **"sad fruit story" family melodrama** (@trombonechef, from 28 Feb 2026): cheating wife → pregnancy → baby reveals the affair (an eggplant baby) → husband's revenge or new marriage, then escalation in numbered "Part N" posts. (b) The **reality-TV parody** (Fruit Love Island by @ai.cinema021, the French "L'Île de la Skibidi Tentafruit" by @onlymoviesfr): a familiar TV format with fruit contestants, daily episodes, tempters/"Casa Amor" twists and audience votes. The hook is the absurd image (a crying pregnant fruit) plus an instantly legible soap-opera conflict. Part 2 can outdraw Part 1 several times over.

### Cited Findings

She gets pregnant, and at delivery the husband sees the baby is an eggplant — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cheating-ai-fruit); [Tribune (PK)](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2599111/cheating-fruit-ai-trend-gains-millions-of-tiktok-views)

  - **Part 3** (2 Mar): the new couple plan a baby, the eggplant boss refuses to help, and the strawberry woman works hard and **buys a gun**. **4.9M+** — [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@trombonechef/video/7612780425877933334)

**Hook formulas and beat structure stated in guides**
- Flashloop's "soap opera formula" (26 Mar 2026) [vendor]:
  - Beat order: **recap → rising tension → dramatic peak → cliffhanger**.
  - Recap opener example: "It had been three months since the wedding..." Peak example: "You're not the father!"
  - Universal hooks: betrayal, jealousy, love triangles, family secrets. "The absurdity itself is the hook": a crying pregnant broccoli is shareable because it is ridiculous, and viewers stay for the story.
  - Source: [Flashloop](https://www.flashloop.ai/blog/how-to-make-ai-fruit-drama-videos)
- Flashloop's example videos (no account names given): a "pregnant broccoli lady in a Manhattan penthouse" (**24.5M**), a "grape villain confronting strawberry ex-wife at a charity gala" (**9M**), and a "broccoli grandmother discovering her husband's secret family" (**8.2M**). Typical episodes get 2–10M views — [Flashloop [vendor]](https://www.flashloop.ai/blog/how-to-make-ai-fruit-drama-videos)
- The cheating-fruit recreation guide orders the five scenes as normal moment → suspicion ("something feels wrong") → discovery/reveal → confrontation → payoff/final reaction. Its example script: a husband comes home happy while his wife is guilty and distant; the wife stands too close to her cucumber boss at work; at the hospital the doctor hands over a cucumber newborn; the wife admits "it was a mistake"; the husband storms down the hallway with the baby to confront the boss — [aicut.pages.dev (ChatGPT + AICUT method)](https://aicut.pages.dev/)
- WOO calls this "Hijacking culturel": take a format audiences already know (*L'Île de la Tentation*, Montoya-style iconic scenes) and inject heavy absurdity. Content that performs well draws on shared cultural memory rather than being original — [WOO Paris, 26 Mar 2026](https://www.woo.paris/blog/lile-de-la-skibidi-tenta-fruit-la-tele-realite-generee-par-ia-qui-explose-tout)

**Non-fruit AI micro-dramas (pets and animals)**
- In China, AI **pet microdramas** came first: cats and dogs living human lives, reenacting regal dramas, **workplace betrayals and rags-to-riches** stories, with cliffhangers.
  - "The Cat Daddy Chronicles" (a cat raising a human baby) has 1M+ followers, and some episodes reached "hundreds of millions" of views.
  - Creators reportedly earn "hundreds of thousands of yuan per month."
  - The trend grew on Douyin and Xiaohongshu.
  - Source: [CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-03-20/AI-Influence-Pet-and-fruit-microdramas-take-the-internet-by-storm-1LEUvn7BBOE/p.html)
- TikTok discover pages show AI animal soap operas (rabbit love triangles, animals reenacting Chinese dramas). Engagement there is modest, often hundreds to low thousands of likes — [TikTok discover: ai-animals-drama](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/ai-animals-drama) (summarized via search; not verified per video)

### Inferences
- The best-performing structure is a **domestic melodrama with a visible paternity "proof"**: the baby looks like the lover's fruit species. The fruit species carries the plot twist, so no exposition is needed and the reveal reads in one frame.
- Trombonechef's Part 2 got about **8×** Part 1's views (24M vs 2.9M). Escalating consequences (eviction, remarriage, revenge set-up) and the curiosity of viewers who missed Part 1 appear to drive more views than the original premise did.
- Reality-TV parodies add two retention levers that plain soaps lack: a **daily appointment** and **audience participation** (votes, "team X" debates).

### Gaps
- I found no sourced data on the exact **first 1–3 seconds** of the top videos (first frame, first line) for Fruit Love Island or trombonechef. Only the Daily Dot line ("This is a pear! Are you cheating on me?") and Flashloop's recap and peak example lines are quoted.
- No sourced per-episode titles or view counts for most Fruit Love Island episodes beyond the averages and the 39M peak (vendor source).
- The @cttr40 "Fruit story" account is mentioned by KYM without premise or views.

## Q2. Episode length, number of shots, seconds per shot, dialogue per shot; how multi-part series are structured

### Takeaway
Most sources put episodes at **60–120 s**, while Fruit Love Island ran longer at **2–4 min**. An episode has **4–6 scenes or beats**, and each beat is one AI generation of about **3–8 s**: Veo 3.1 clips are 4, 6 or 8 s, and most models cap at 5–10 s. Voiceover or dialogue per scene is **about 5–8 s, roughly 80–90 characters**, kept "short and sharp." Every episode ends on an unresolved cliffhanger shot, and series are posted **daily**.

### Cited Findings
- **Fruit Love Island**: episodes of **2–4 minutes**, 2 seasons. Episode counts conflict: 28 per Wikipedia, 22 or 23 per other reports — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit_Love_Island); [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/black-creator-says-ai-fruit-164743347.html)
- Fruit and food micro-drama episodes "typically run **90 seconds to two minutes**," and cliffhangers encourage binge-watching — [CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-03-20/AI-Influence-Pet-and-fruit-microdramas-take-the-internet-by-storm-1LEUvn7BBOE/p.html)
- Flashloop's numbers [vendor]:
  - **1–2 minute** episodes with **4–6 scenes**.
  - Each scene is **one AI image animated into a 3–5 s clip**.
  - Voiceover is about **5–8 s per scene, roughly 80–90 characters**.
  - Narration should complement the visuals, not describe them, in a "true-crime documentary or telenovela" tone. Suggested TTS: ElevenLabs or PlayHT.
  - The final scene ends on a cliffhanger (a ringing phone, an opening door, a positive test): "Never resolve everything."
  - End-card text: "Part [X] tomorrow..." or "follow for the next episode."
  - **Subtitles mandatory** because many viewers watch muted.
  - Recurring cast of **5–10 characters**.
  - Source: [Flashloop](https://www.flashloop.ai/blog/how-to-make-ai-fruit-drama-videos)
  - Note: 4–6 scenes × 5–8 s of voiceover adds up to only 20–48 s, which is internally inconsistent with 1–2 min episodes. More scenes or longer scenes are needed for a 1–2 min runtime.
- The cheating-fruit recreation example uses **5 scenes**, with "usually every major cut becomes one new scene" — [aicut.pages.dev](https://aicut.pages.dev/)
- AI micro-drama grammar per invideo [vendor]:
  - **60–120 s per episode**, "one dramatic beat per episode."
  - Every episode ends on a reveal, reversal or interrupted action, written in at script stage so "the shot list should already know which shot is the cliffhanger."
  - "a 4-minute episode is a short film wearing the wrong clothes."
  - A documented production made 10 episodes of 1.5–2 min in 3 days with 3 people at about $1,000 per episode.
  - Source: [invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/how-to-make-ai-micro-drama/)
- Retention architecture from the qwe.edu.pl guide:
  - "plants a major hook every **45–60 seconds** and ends every episode on a cliffhanger that **resolves in the first 10 seconds of the next** – then plants a new hook in the **final 5**."
  - "Each beat = one shot = one AI generation."
  - Prose scripts produce inconsistent shot lengths ("some generate 3 seconds of footage, some 12. Pacing collapses").
  - "Most models cap at 5–10 seconds per generation."
  - Edit with "hard-baked captions (most viewers watch muted)… Music low, dialogue loud, **cliffhanger sting on the last frame**."
  - Source: [qwe.edu.pl](https://www.qwe.edu.pl/tutorial/short-dramas-ai-beginners-guide/)
- Further timing and series advice [vendor]:
  - Episodes of **60–90 s**, posted **daily**, with a season arc of **5–10 episodes** resolving at the finale.
  - Fan-camp comment debates ("Team Strawberry" vs "Team Banana").
  - Suggests TikTok Series paywalls of $0.99–$4.99 per episode.
  - Source: [MuleRun](https://blog.mulerun.com/p/how-to-make-ai-micro-dramas-for-tiktok-and-reels-2026/)
- **Veo 3.1 specs** ("verified January 2026"):
  - Clip duration 4, 6 or 8 s. 9:16 supported, 24 fps.
  - Up to 3 reference images, native dialogue, SFX and ambience.
  - Limitations: "use one major action per shot"; "Exact lip-sync is not guaranteed."
  - Source: [snubroot/Veo-3-Meta-Framework](https://github.com/snubroot/Veo-3-Meta-Framework)
- A Veo-oriented guide suggests building longer pieces as a **3–6 clip shot list** stitched together — [Quest Studio](https://queststudio.io/blog/veo-3-prompt-guide) (via search summary)
- The shotlist-forge example uses **4 s per shot**. It groups shots into generation passes capped at 30 s (Seedance 2.5) or 15 s (Seedance 2.0) — [shotlist-forge](https://github.com/thoxakihiko/shotlist-forge)
- **Multi-part mechanics observed**:
  - Numbered "Part 1/2/3" posts on consecutive days (28 Feb, 1 Mar, 2 Mar). Each part ends on a new problem (eviction, the boss refusing to help, the gun purchase) — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cheating-ai-fruit)
  - Fruit Love Island used a **daily cadence** — [How It Spread](https://howitspread.com/cases/fruit-love-island)
  - It also had numbered multi-part event arcs ("Boys Casa Amor Part One") — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/viral/ai-fruit-love-island-tiktok-slop-popularity-rcna264946)
  - The French version had 12 numbered episodes, and a "nouveau tentateur" (new tempter) arrived in episode 9 — [Presse-citron](https://www.presse-citron.net/cest-nul-mais-tout-le-monde-regarde-les-fruits-ia-explosent-les-compteurs-sur-tiktok/)
  - TikTok has a discover page "Part Two of The Ai Fruit," reportedly updated 5 Oct 2026 — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/part-two-of-the-ai-fruit) (via search summary)
- **Villain and recurring cast**:
  - The eggplant boss (the affair partner who later refuses to help) and the orange "new wife" serve as recurring antagonists — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cheating-ai-fruit)
  - Flashloop lists **revenge arcs built over several episodes** and a "grape villain" archetype — [Flashloop](https://www.flashloop.ai/blog/how-to-make-ai-fruit-drama-videos)
- **Dialogue rules** in the ChatGPT meta-prompt: "keep lines short / keep lines sharp / keep lines emotionally clear / keep lines instantly understandable … if silence is stronger, keep the scene silent." It also specifies "only the active speaker's mouth moves" — [aicut.pages.dev](https://aicut.pages.dev/)

### Inferences
- A practical spec consistent with all sources: a **65–90 s episode** (to clear TikTok's >1 min Creator Rewards threshold, see Q5) with **8–15 shots of 4–8 s**. That means one beat per shot, one line of 6–15 words per speaking shot, burned-in captions, and a final 3–5 s cliffhanger shot with a sound "sting." The first 2–3 s of the next episode should resolve or recap the cliffhanger.
- The "4–6 scenes" figure in fruit-drama guides refers to **story scenes**. Each scene may be cut into 2–3 shots, which reconciles 4–6 scenes with 1–2 min runtimes.

### Gaps
- No frame-by-frame breakdown (shot count, average shot length) of an actual top Fruit Love Island or trombonechef episode was found. The figures above come from guides, mostly vendor guides.
- The exact runtime of trombonechef's parts is not reported.
- No sourced words-per-second or words-per-line metric specific to these accounts. Flashloop's 80–90 characters per 5–8 s scene is the only quantitative figure.

## Q3. Best-performing themes and tropes, and what is trending now (Oct 2026); French-speaking market; music and sound

### Takeaway
**Infidelity plus a pregnancy or paternity reveal** is the proven top trope, followed by family-dynasty/inheritance fights, multi-episode revenge, secret identities, and reality-TV couple-swap formats (tempters, Casa Amor). Wealth settings (a penthouse, a charity gala) recur in high-view examples. In France, **@onlymoviesfr's "L'Île de la Skibidi Tentafruit"** (an *Île de la Tentation* parody) reached about 3M followers in about 9 days with millions of views per episode and attracted brands (Oasis, Danette, Free, Picard). I found **no reliable evidence of what is specifically trending in Sep–Oct 2026**; the latest documented wave is spring 2026.

## Q5. Platform rules: TikTok AI labeling, unoriginal/mass AI content, monetization (Creator Rewards >1 min), 2026 changes

### Takeaway
TikTok requires labeling of **realistic** AI-generated content. It auto-detects AI content via C2PA Content Credentials and, since late 2025, invisible watermarks on content made with TikTok tools, and it lets users **turn down AI content in their feed** (since Nov 2025). Unoriginal, reused or watermarked content and repetitive automated posting are excluded from the For You feed and from Creator Rewards. Creator Rewards needs **10k followers, 100k views in 30 days, age 18+, and videos over 1 minute** with qualified views; France is an eligible country in both lists found. Labeled, original AI content is reportedly still eligible. Most monetization details come from third-party guides, not TikTok's own pages, which I could not load.

### Cited Findings
